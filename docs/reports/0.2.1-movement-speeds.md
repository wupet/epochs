# Milestone 0.2.1: Movement speeds

Follow-ups to 0.2, from your decisions of 2026-11-19. The design is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-movement-speeds-design.md`, and your decisions are recorded in the brief and the design summary.

## What changed

- **Speeds.**
  - Walking is now one tile every 2 ticks (2 tiles/s).
  - Any speed works: each tick a character builds up progress and takes a step for each whole tile, up to 2 tiles in one tick. The numbers are whole thousandths of a tile, so it stays exactly deterministic.
  - **No free steps.** Standing still banks at most one tick's worth of movement at your current speed (never less than one step, so a rested walker still steps on the next tick). Every speed sets off at full pace, and spam-clicking can't beat your pace. Tests click back and forth for 40 ticks at walk, run and horse to check this. *(Updated 2026-11-19 after your playtest: it used to cap at one step.)*
- **Debug speed keys:** **1** walk (2 tiles/s), **2** run (4), **3** horse (8). They are labelled debug in the code, the status line and the docs. Running and riding get real rules later.
- **Paths** now follow the straight line between start and end as closely as possible, instead of going diagonal first.
- **Unreachable clicks** go to the nearest reachable tile, where a diagonal counts as 1 step away. The search stays within 16 tiles of the click, about one screen. With nothing reachable in range, you stay put.
- **Slides** are timed from each character's speed: 500 ms per tile when walking, so walking doesn't stop and start. A rider moving 2 tiles in a tick slides through the tile in between instead of cutting the corner.
- **Zoom:** **+** (or **=**) and **−**, plus **0** to go back to the whole map. Zoomed in, the camera follows your square and stops at the map edges.
- **Stalls:** the server catches up at most 4 ticks (1 s), skips the rest, and logs `[epochs] stalled: skipped N ticks`.
- **A full server:** at most 100 players. Anyone after that waits in a queue showing "In queue: position N" and joins when a slot frees.
- **The tick pulse** is off by default. **P** turns it on, and `?pulse=1` starts with it on.
- **Docs:** CLAUDE.md "Key numbers", the brief, the design summary and the technical plan's world-size note now say walking is 2 tiles/s, and that a tile is about 1 m.
- **Tests:** 245 in 19 files, all passing, and the typecheck is clean.

## How to check it by hand

Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:5173.

1. **Walk** somewhere. The square moves at half its old pace, sliding continuously.
2. Press **2** and click: it runs at the old pace. Press **3**: it moves twice as fast again, and the slide still follows the path around corners. Press **1** to walk.
3. **Spam-click** two neighbouring tiles at walk speed. It never moves faster than walking.
4. **Click across open ground** at an angle. The path is a near-straight staircase, not a diagonal followed by a straight run.
5. Press **+** a few times. The camera follows your square, hovering still shows the right tile as the map scrolls, and clicks land where you point. **0** fits the whole map again.
6. **Queue:** this needs 101 connections, so it's easiest through the tests (`npm test`). In the browser I opened 99 extra connections from the console and loaded a second tab. It showed "In queue: position 1" and got in when one connection closed.

## What I measured

These were measured in Chrome, using a second watch-only connection to log every tile the square stepped on.

| Mode | Measured | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | Steps on every second tick, 6 tiles in 12 ticks | 2 tiles/s |
| Run | A step every tick | 4 tiles/s |
| Horse | 1 tile, then 2 every tick, each message listing the tile passed (measured before the 2026-11-19 rest-bank change; a rested rider now moves 2 on the first tick) | 8 tiles/s |
| Spam-clicking at walk | Never on consecutive ticks | Never faster than walking |
| Queue | Positions 1 and 2. When a player left, the first got in and the second became 1 | — |

There were no errors in the browser or the server log. Smoothness still has to be judged by eye: Chrome slows animation in the window I drive, as in 0.2.

A few notes on the measurements and fixes:

- **The spam check in the browser alternated on a fixed schedule,** so some clicks targeted the tile the square was already on. The automated test is sharper: each tick it clicks whichever of two neighbouring tiles you're not on. At walk that gives exactly 20 steps in 40 ticks, the walking pace, never two in a row. At run and horse it gives one step per tick. A second version alternates between two tiles 6 apart, and every speed is checked against the most it could earn.
- **I didn't re-check clicking inside the closed room in the browser** this time. The route tests cover nearest-reachable, the 16-tile range and the tie-breaks, and the room behaved correctly in 0.2.
- **The final review found a click problem, now fixed:** while the camera was scrolling, a click could land one tile off from the highlighted tile. Clicks now use exactly the layout on screen.
- **It also found that slides can cut a corner when ticks arrive in a burst,** for example right after a stall or when a backgrounded tab catches up. This predates this milestone. I tried two fixes and each broke something worse:
  - keeping the in-between tiles made the square fall several tiles behind during normal walking;
  - timing by distance made it replay every missed tile after a burst.

  So the slide code is back to the reviewed version, which stays about one tile behind and catches up within one tile's time. Two new tests guard that: one with ticks arriving up to 15 ms early or late, one with a burst of 40 ticks. See design question 5.

## What to judge by feel

- **Does walking feel right at 2 tiles/s,** or too slow with this map size?
- **Is the slide smooth at each speed?** If it hitches at each tile, raise `SLIDE_TIME_SCALE` in `packages/client/src/motion.ts` slightly, to about 1.08.
- **Do the new paths look natural?** Near walls they still have to bend. For example, the horse route above went through the gap in a wall.
- **Are the zoom steps and following camera comfortable?** The tile sizes are 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48 and 64 px.

## Known issues

- **A failing tick retries forever** (from 0.2). It's scheduled first thing in 0.3, as you asked.
- **The waiting queue has no size limit.** One client could open thousands of connections. That's fine on localhost, but it needs a cap before public hosting, along with the earlier hosting list.

## Design questions

1. **A click with nothing reachable in range stops you.** If you're walking and click somewhere more than 16 tiles from any reachable tile, the click replaces your path with "stay here", so you stop. Should it be ignored instead, so you keep walking?
2. **A rider's first tick.** *Resolved (Peter, 2026-11-19): a rested rider now sets off at 2 tiles a tick.* Resting banks one tick's worth of movement at your current speed, never less than one step. A character that joins and switches to horse in the same tick still starts with one step banked, so its first tick is one tile; any rest tick in horse mode fixes that.
3. **Switching speed mid-walk.** Switching from horse to walk mid-walk carries the horse's leftover progress, so you get one 2-tile tick before slowing down. The keys are debug-only, so I left it. Is that worth tidying when speeds get real rules?
4. **Queue messages.** The queue shows only your position. Do you also want how many players are online, or a rough wait time?
5. **Smooth movement through bursts.** The proper fix for corner-cutting after a burst is to draw everyone from the server's recent tick history at a small fixed delay, instead of sliding toward each new tile as it arrives. That is perfectly smooth and never cuts corners, but everything you see, including your own square, would be about one tick (250 ms) further behind your clicks. Do you want that in 0.3, or keep today's more responsive slides with the occasional corner cut after a hiccup?
