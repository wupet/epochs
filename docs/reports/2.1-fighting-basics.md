# Milestone 2.1: Fighting basics

You can now walk up to a training dummy and a slime, attack them, take damage, die, and read a recap that explains why. The design is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-fighting-basics-design.md`. Its "builder's calls" (the small decisions I made where the brief was silent) are listed under "Things to know" below, for you to confirm.

## What was built

- **Arena and content.**
  - A 24 by 24 arena with pillars and a campfire. Players join and respawn on the tile next to the campfire.
  - Content files for everything new: four weapons (shortsword, cudgel, hunting bow, witch's staff), the leather jerkin (armor 1), the slime, the training dummy, three class presets (Militia, Hunter, Hedge Witch), the arena, and two waves (the practice setup and wave 1).
  - The slime is 12 health, with a Touch attack: a 1-tick windup, a 3-tick wait afterwards, 3 Force, 0.5 Precision and 1.25 Arcane resistances, and a walking speed of 500. The dummy is 1000 health, never moves or attacks, and heals to full 20 ticks after its last hit.
  - The server loads and checks `content/` when it starts, and refuses to start if anything is wrong.
- **Character sheets.** Level 1 to 10, the five attributes, health (30 + 5 per level after the first + 3 per Vigor), and the point from each level going to the preset's main attribute.
- **The damage formula,** exactly as you settled it: a random spread of plus or minus 10%, attribute and level bonuses, resistances, armor (never more than 90% of the damage), whole numbers with half rounded up, at least 1 damage, and 0 against a target that is immune. Randomness comes from the world's seeded generator, so the same seed and the same inputs give the same fight.
- **Fighting.**
  - Click an enemy to chase and attack it. Press **A**, then click a tile, to attack-move. Esc or a right-click cancels.
  - A basic attack lands on the first tick you are in reach, then waits the weapon's interval (4 or 5 ticks). Walking away doesn't reset the wait.
  - Enemies block movement, and enemies can't enter a tile with a player on it. Players pass through each other.
  - Reach follows your corner rule: no diagonal strikes past a pillar's corner, and ranged shots need a clear line between tile centres.
- **Placeholder enemy AI.** Slimes chase whoever hurt them last, or else the nearest player, and touch whoever is standing on the telegraphed tile when the windup ends. The AI lives in one small file (`enemy-ai.ts`) so it is easy to replace later.
- **Death.** You are down for one tick, then respawn at the campfire at full health, with no penalty. Enemies whose last damager was you forget you. The death recap lists your last 5 hits: who, with what, how much, and the telegraph you were standing in.
- **What the client sees.** Enemies coloured by family, health bars, telegraph tiles (filled and outlined in the damage kind's colour), floating damage numbers for hits you deal and take, and the campfire. The server still sends each player only what they can see: resistances, orders and paths never leave the server.
- **A debug panel** (plain HTML, on the right of the page): pick a preset, level 1 to 10, move attribute points with +/−, pick a weapon and armor, set class ranks, heal to full, start the practice setup or wave 1, and clear enemies. Debug messages are marked as debug in the code, and capped at 8 per player per tick.
- **Seeds.** The server logs `[epochs] seed <n>` at startup and accepts `EPOCHS_SEED=<n>` to replay a run.
- **Tests:** 634 in 39 files, all passing. The typecheck is clean, and `npm run check-content` says `All 15 content files are valid.`

## How to run it

```
npm ci                      # once after merging: the server now depends on @epochs/tools
npm run dev                 # client at http://localhost:5173
npm test
npm run check-content       # All 15 content files are valid.
```

`npm ci` is needed after the merge because the server now uses `@epochs/tools` (to load content), so `package-lock.json` changed.

To replay a run, set the seed first. In Git Bash: `EPOCHS_SEED=12345 npm run dev`. In PowerShell: `$env:EPOCHS_SEED = "12345"; npm run dev`. With no seed set, the server picks one and logs it.

## What to check by hand

Start `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:5173. You should see the arena, the campfire (an orange circle), your square next to it, and the debug panel on the right (Militia, level 1, Rusty Shortsword, Leather Jerkin).

1. **Practice setup.** Click "Start Practice: dummy and slime". The brown dummy appears at one end and a green slime at the other, both with health bars.
2. **Take a hit.** The slime walks straight to you. An orange outline appears on your tile (the Touch telegraph). If you are still standing on it a tick later, you take 2 damage (a "2" floats up, and your health bar appears). A 1-tick windup is meant to be avoided by positioning, not by reaction (see the design questions below).
3. **Kill the slime.** Click it. You walk to it and attack. With the shortsword it takes two or three hits (12 health, 5 or 6 per hit).
4. **Attack the dummy.** Click it, or press **A** and then click a tile next to it. You attack and a "6" or "7" floats up. Its 1000-health bar barely moves. Stop attacking (click a tile to walk away; attack orders and locked attack-moves don't end on their own) and wait 5 seconds (20 ticks): it heals to full.
5. **Change gear.** In the panel, pick Hunter or the hunting bow, then attack the dummy from a distance. Hedge Witch and the staff work the same way. Changing anything on the sheet heals you to full.
6. **Reach the recap quickly.** A slime does only 2 damage per touch through the jerkin, so a Militia (36 health) takes about 22 seconds to die to one slime. To speed it up, set armor to "none" (3 damage per touch), or click "Start Wave 1" (three slimes), or both.
7. **Read the recap.** When you die, a "You died" overlay lists your last hits, for example `Slime, Touch, 3 damage, you were standing in its Touch telegraph (1-tick windup)`. It stays until you click Close or press Esc. You are back at the campfire at full health.
8. **Class ranks.** The class-rank control lists only Militia for now. The Hunter and Hedge Witch class files arrive in 2.2, although all three presets ship now.
9. **Cancel.** Press A, then Esc (or right-click): the attack-move is cancelled.
10. **Two tabs** (optional): open a second tab. Both players see each other, and slimes go for whoever is nearest. This was not checked by hand (see below).

I walked through steps 1 to 4, 6, 7 and 9 in Chrome with `EPOCHS_SEED=12345`, and they worked, with no console errors. I did not check by hand: the zoomed-in camera with enemies, two players in two tabs, and ranged weapons in the browser (tests cover ranged weapons).

## Things to know

These are the calls I made where the brief was silent. Please confirm or change any of them.

**From the design document (calls 1 to 14):**

1. **Tick order** is fixed: joins and respawns, debug intentions, movement modes, orders, enemy orders, paths, movement, combat, deaths, then dummy healing. Within each step, players go in id order, then enemies.
2. **Hits within a tick land one after another,** and deaths are handled at the end of the tick. A character at 0 health or below stops acting for the rest of the tick, but can still be hit.
3. **Down for one tick.** A player who dies on tick T respawns on T+1. While down they can't act, block, be targeted or be hit. The tick that reports the killing hit still shows them where they fell, and the next one shows them at the campfire.
4. **Slime cadence.** A touch starts on tick T and resolves on T+1. The 3-tick wait is T+2 to T+4, and the next touch can start on T+5.
5. **Weapon cadence.** A swing on tick T allows the next swing on T plus the weapon's interval.
6. **Ranges use the same distance as movement:** a diagonal neighbour is 1 away. Melee is range 1. Anything with range above 1 also needs line of sight.
7. **Blocked steps.** When an occupied tile blocks your next step, you re-route around the occupants once. If there is still no legal step, you wait and keep your progress.
8. **Enemies don't block line of sight.** Only pillars and other blocked tiles do.
9. **Arena, waves and presets are content files.** The campfire is a blocked tile. The spawn tile beside it is where players appear and respawn.
10. **Holy maps to Spirit** in the damage bonuses, following the design summary. Holy is not used in Phase 2.
11. **Rounding is half up,** and everything is in whole numbers. The spread comes from the seeded generator.
12. **The death recap shows the last 5 hits** you took.
13. **Debug sheet changes heal you to full:** preset, level, point moves, weapon, armor and class rank.
14. **Debug intentions are capped** at 8 per player per tick.

**Other choices made during the build:**

- **A blocked mover waits with at most one tick's worth of progress.** Waiting never builds up a free step, so when the way clears you don't lurch forward.
- **A diagonal step past even one occupied tile is refused.** It is the same corner rule as pillars, so the hover preview matches what the server will do.
- **Chasing walks to the nearest tile from which the target is in reach.** This is needed for corners and line of sight (for example, a ranged weapon that must step out from behind a pillar).
- **A ranged weapon can't shoot a diagonal neighbour around a pillar corner.** This is your line-of-sight corner rule applied to a very short shot.
- **Down players may still use the debug panel.** The change applies when they respawn a tick later. It is debug-only.
- **An idle enemy (no players left) finishes its current path** instead of stopping on the spot.
- **A slime won't start a touch on a player who has just been knocked to 0 health** (builder's call), so a touch never lands on you right after you respawn, even when you respawn on the tile it was aiming at.
- **The debug catalog now tells the client each preset's main attribute and each weapon's range.** The panel needs the first to lock the main attribute's "−/+" row, and hover previews need the second. Neither is hidden information.
- **Clicking an enemy picks the body as it is drawn** (mid-slide), falling back to the tile it is on by the server's latest position. The hover outline and path preview follow the same rule.
- **Downed players are drawn faded** for their one tick, to make deaths readable.
- **The page got a little heavier.** The client's main script grew from about 80.5 KB to 95.2 KB compressed, mostly PixiJS text for the damage numbers (about 11.6 KB). There is still no Zod in the client.

**Housekeeping:** the code reviews turned up a list of small code-quality points (duplicated helpers, a few weak tests, some long files). None affect behaviour. They are tracked for the final review of the milestone.

## Design questions

1. **Respawn camping.** After you respawn, the slimes chase the nearest player, and that is you, beside the campfire. With no healing and no invulnerability, I saw three slimes gather around the campfire, which could turn into a loop: die, respawn beside the slimes, die again. Options: a short grace period after respawn, enemies resetting when you die, or leave it for the wave rules.
2. **Does dodging feel fair?** You asked for this one explicitly. The slime's 1-tick touch is meant to be avoided by positioning, not reaction: at walking pace, whether you can step out in time depends on where you are in your stride. Please play it and say whether that feels fair or just random. Anything meant to be dodged by reacting must have a windup of 2 ticks or more, as you ruled.
3. **How slime damage feels through the jerkin.** The jerkin (armor 1) cuts a touch from 3 to 2. A Militia has 36 health, so one slime takes about 22 seconds to kill you, and a wave of three slimes does it in a few seconds. Is 2 per touch about right?
4. **Campfire healing.** There is none yet, and no regeneration. The panel has a full-heal button. Campfire healing waits for your design, and it interacts with the respawn question above.
5. **What class ranks will do.** The panel has a rank control (1 to 3), and a rank is saved on the sheet, but it changes nothing in 2.1. Its meaning arrives with the classes in 2.2. Is there anything you want the rank to do before then?
